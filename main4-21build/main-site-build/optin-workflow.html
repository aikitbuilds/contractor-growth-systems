Toll-Free Number Opt-In Workflow

This document outlines the complete opt-in process for our toll-free number messaging and email communications.

Email & Text Message Opt-In Process

1

Website Form Submission

Users complete our contact form on the website with clear opt-in language for both email and SMS communications.

✓ Yes, I'd like to receive occasional text messages with tips, offers and updates from Billion Dollar Contractor. (Message & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel at any time.)

✓ I'd like to receive email communications from Billion Dollar Contractor with valuable resources and offers.

2

Double Opt-In Confirmation

After form submission, the user receives:

  • Email confirmation requiring them to click a link to confirm subscription
  • Text message confirmation requiring a reply of "YES" to confirm SMS subscription

SMS: "Reply YES to confirm your subscription to Billion Dollar Contractor alerts. Max 4 msgs/month. Reply HELP for help, STOP to cancel. Msg&data rates may apply."

3

User Confirmation

The user must take active steps to complete both opt-ins:

  • Click the confirmation link in the email
  • Reply "YES" to the SMS confirmation message

Only after these explicit confirmations are users added to our communication lists.

4

Welcome Message & Unsubscribe Instructions

Upon confirmation, users receive welcome messages that include:

  • Confirmation of successful opt-in
  • Clear instructions for how to unsubscribe at any time
  • Expected frequency of communications
  • Link to privacy policy

SMS: "Welcome to Billion Dollar Contractor! You're all set to receive updates & offers. Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help. Msg&data rates may apply. Privacy policy: billiondollarcontractor.com/privacy"

5

Ongoing Communication Opt-Out Options

All future communications include:

  • Email: Unsubscribe link in every email
  • SMS: Instructions to reply "STOP" to cancel in every text message

Opt-out requests are processed immediately and automatically.
